Permanent QR Lost & Found

Scan a Scan2SafeIndia sticker to register your item or, if you found the item, to see the owner's chosen contact options.

How it works

1. Buy one permanent QR sticker.
2. Scan it and register your item.
3. If the item is lost, a finder scans the same QR.
4. The finder sees only the contact details you chose to share.

Privacy

Do not enter a home address, Aadhaar number, password, OTP or other sensitive information in the public item details. Only share information needed to return the item.
